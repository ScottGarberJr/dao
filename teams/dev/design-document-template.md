# Project design document template

Use this template as the default `project/docs/design.md` in every project. It is the UI/UX and Figma-facing design document that planning and building follow.

For a larger engagement or when a client requests it, split individual sections into `project/docs/design/`. Keep `project/docs/design.md` as the concise index, decision summary, and link map to those files; it remains the entry point for agents.

Complete only sections that apply. Mark a deliberately deferred item **TBD** and put unanswered decisions in **Open questions**. Do not treat template guidance as project facts.

## UI

### Branding

Record logo use, voice, imagery direction, iconography, component/system constraints, and client brand requirements.

### Colors

Record the approved palette, semantic color roles, states, contrast requirements, and Figma variable or style references.

### Typography

Record font choices, hierarchy, scale, responsive behavior, and Figma style references.

### Design system

Record the component library, tokens, reusable patterns, states, accessibility requirements, and the relationship between Figma components and production components.

### Visual direction

Record the chosen direction, key composition and layout decisions, imagery or illustration guidance, responsive behavior, and the rationale from discovery and review.

## UX

### User personas

Define relevant users, their goals, needs, contexts, accessibility considerations, and meaningful permissions.

### User flows

Map key tasks from entry through success, failure, empty, loading, and edge states. Link to Figma flow diagrams or prototype frames.

### Interaction and responsive behavior

Record interaction patterns, feedback, motion, breakpoints, platform differences, and usability constraints.

## Figma and references

Link the Figma file; designated design pages and frames; discovery/reference pages; sketches; brand assets; approved external references; and the related `requirements.md` and `architecture.md`.

## Open questions

List decisions still needed, their owner, impact, and the decision required before planning, building, or release.

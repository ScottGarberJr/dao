# Project requirements document template

Use this template as the default `project/docs/requirements.md` in every project. It records the product or site’s functional overview and scope.

Complete only sections that apply. Mark a deliberately deferred item **TBD** and put unanswered decisions in **Open questions**. Do not treat template guidance as project facts.

## Core idea, objective, and themes

Describe the product or site’s core idea, intended users, user and business objective, and the product, editorial, or experience themes. State the primary outcome the work must support.

## Features

### Version 1

List the features and boundaries required for the current version. Describe functional behavior, important rules, and the intended outcome for each item. Link material items to their version-plan section once it exists.

### Immediates

List the next agreed work that may affect current decisions but is not part of the current build.

### Additionals

List longer-term, optional, or exploratory ideas separately so they do not silently expand Version 1.

## Strategy

### Competitors

List competitor or comparable-product findings that influence scope or product direction. Capture the lesson and reference; do not copy their implementation or brand.

### Content

Define content types, ownership, required copy, media needs, editorial workflow, and empty or error-state content.

### SEO

For sites, define search intent, target topics, information architecture, metadata, structured data, technical SEO, and measurement. Mark it not applicable for a native app unless there is web-discoverable content.

### Marketing

Record audiences, acquisition paths, campaign or landing-page needs, analytics events, and conversion measurement.

### Monetization

Record pricing, subscriptions, commerce, entitlements, upgrade paths, and related user-facing rules.

### Policy

Record privacy, terms, consent, accessibility, platform, client, or industry requirements affecting what the product must do. Obtain legal review when required; this document is not legal advice.

## References

Link discovery, client brief, approved external references, the Figma design document, architecture document, and related plans.

## Open questions

List decisions still needed, their owner, impact, and the decision required before planning, building, or release.

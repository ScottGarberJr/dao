# Project design planning template

Use this template for the planning and direction sections of `project/docs/design.md`. Keep the main document concise and link larger evidence in `project/docs/design/`.

## Brief

### Users

Who is this for? Record relevant context, goals, accessibility needs, and permissions.

### Problem and desired outcome

What problem is being solved, and what should be different after the work?

### Primary tasks

List the tasks or decisions the design must support.

### Scope

Include the screens, flows, sections, or states covered by this design.

### Non-goals

Record what is deliberately outside the design.

### Constraints

Record product, technical, content, brand, platform, accessibility, legal, and schedule constraints.

## Selected direction

### Direction summary

Describe the chosen visual and interaction direction in implementation-facing terms.

### Rationale

Explain how the direction responds to discovery evidence, user needs, and project constraints.

### Alternatives considered

Link or summarize rejected, deferred, or exploratory alternatives and why they were not selected.

### Layout and interaction decisions

Record hierarchy, navigation, density, responsive behavior, important states, and interaction patterns. Use a named interaction model only when it clarifies the screen’s primary user activity; do not force a category where it does not help.

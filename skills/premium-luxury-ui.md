# Premium/Luxury UI

## Purpose

Guide a restrained, intentional premium direction through typography, spacing, materials, imagery, and pacing without reducing usability.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the audience, brand posture, product promise, and approved direction.
2. Use purposeful typography, whitespace, material contrast, and carefully selected imagery.
3. Keep content realistic and verify responsive, accessibility, and interaction states.
4. Use motion sparingly and for progression or emphasis.
5. Run Design Review before polish.

## Boundaries

Do not equate luxury with low contrast, empty decoration, expensive assets, or inaccessible interactions.
